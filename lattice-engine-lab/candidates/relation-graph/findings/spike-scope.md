# Relation Graph XMind and Editable Associations Spike

The spike evaluates a distinct responsibility: an Editable Association and Mapping Workspace. It does not replace React Flow, Graphology, ELK, or the canonical Lattice graph.

The graph begins with a central Screwjack product and stable Teamcenter, Windchill, SAP, and Configit branches. Controlled actions reveal product structure, spindle occurrence, canonical spindle, Windchill context, requirement, change, document, SAP operational and financial evidence, Configit logic, Excel provenance, and unresolved evidence.

User-created relationships are stored separately from source facts with state, author, timestamps, rationale, investigation scope, and evidence classification. The laboratory does not implement persistence, authentication, authorization, versioning, conflict resolution, review workflow, or source-system write-back.
