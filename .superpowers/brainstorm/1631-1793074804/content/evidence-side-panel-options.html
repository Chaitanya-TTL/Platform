A · Expand the workbench

Opening Evidence or Review suggestions widens the Copilot workspace and places one contextual panel beside the transcript. The graph remains visible behind it.

Benefit: chat keeps readable width; evidence stays adjacent. Cost: a larger overlay changes more of the screen. On mobile, the evidence view replaces chat until closed.

B · Keep the 520px drawer

Chat, Evidence, and Review are separate views inside the existing Copilot drawer; opening either switches the drawer away from the transcript and a Chat tab returns you.

Benefit: calmer, familiar footprint and no extra page scroll. Cost: evidence is no longer visible beside its answer; users switch views to compare them.
