# Three-Question Market Slide Design

## Intent

Replace the narrative-heavy market slide with concise slide-ready content that
directly answers the senior stakeholder's three questions: what is unique about
the offering, what value it adds for customers, and what existing tools overlap.
Keep the separate deployment-resource slide unchanged.

## Goals

- Use the three questions as the slide's organizing headings.
- Explain the proposed offering as engineering-domain context connected across
  PLM, ERP, requirements/ALM, and product-configuration systems, supporting
  evidence-grounded answers and a longer-term path to governed actions.
- State customer value in plain language without presenting unmeasured outcomes
  as proven.
- Name relevant existing offerings and summarize their documented overlap using
  the official sources already recorded in the deliverable.
- Distinguish current answer capability from future, human-approved write-back.

## Non-goals and constraints

- Do not edit Slide 5 or the PowerPoint deck.
- Do not claim model training, autonomous changes, implemented write-back, or
  proven market uniqueness.
- Do not claim competitors lack cross-system capabilities.
- Keep the slide concise; detailed source URLs and caveats may remain in notes
  beneath the slide copy.

## Approaches

1. **Three direct question headings (selected):** concise and maps directly to
   the stakeholder's request; keeps the deployment slide separate.
2. **Short speaker-note response:** simpler to present verbally but provides no
   self-contained slide copy.
3. **Retain a shortened capability journey:** preserves the current narrative
   but does not answer the three questions as directly.

The revised market slide will use the selected first approach. The main copy
will distinguish today's evidence-grounded answering from longer-term,
human-approved source-system actions. Competitor descriptions will remain
limited to capabilities supported by the official vendor links already in the
deliverable.
