# Identity and Provenance Rules

- IDs are deterministic from kind, source, native identity, and relationship endpoints.
- Source namespaces prevent cross-application ID collisions.
- Canonical part and part-occurrence identities are separate kinds.
- Identity quality is native, deterministic-derived, or provisional.
- Source facts use source-authoritative authority. Assemblers and evaluators use backend-derived authority.
- Compatibility data may be frontend-derived, hardcoded, or mocked, and must never be promoted silently.
- Inferences require confidence and evidence references.
- Findings require provenance and affected entity references.
